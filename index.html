Nitro Surge Fighters is an addictive, high-energy fighting game built around speed, power, and the thrill of one more round.

Nitro brings the velocity. Surge unleashes overwhelming force. Rage pushes every fighter beyond their limits—with even more power-ups on the way.

Rooted in the bold spirit of 1980s video games, NSF pairs pick-up-and-play arcade action with modern combat design, expressive fighters, and combo depth that rewards timing and mastery.

Play free in your browser on PC or mobile, no download needed: 8 fighters, Arcade and Versus battles, the Beat Them All survival run, the Cyber Cab Smash bonus stage, Punching Ball combo training and global leaderboards.

ARCADE MODE · UNLOCK SPECIAL MODES

How to earn Nitro Coins

  1. 01
    Land your hits

    Every hit that connects earns Nitro Coins. The harder it hits, the more you get.

    1 HIT = 1 NITRO COIN · HEAVY HITS = MORE
  2. 02
    Chain combos

    From the 2nd hit of a combo, all its coins are doubled. Long combos pay big!

    MINIMUM 2-HIT COMBO x 2 = 4 NITRO COINS
  3. 03
    Win the fight

    Win the round and every coin earned during the fight is added to your total.

    WIN = 100% OF YOUR FIGHT COINS
  4. 04
    Difficulty bonus

    Each victory adds a bonus: NORMAL +100 · HARD +200 · EXTREME +300.

    UP TO +300 NITRO COINS PER WIN

Coins are earned in ARCADE mode (not in TRAINING). Spend them to unlock the Special Modes.
